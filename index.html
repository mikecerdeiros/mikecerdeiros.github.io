Designed for the senses

A quieter way to count down.

Feel every second

Synchronized Core Haptics give each visible detent a precise response. Below one minute, choose any whole second; longer timers settle on :00 or :30.

Listen in space

With compatible headphones, quiet binaural clicks follow your touch around the dial. Speaker playback remains restrained and balanced.

Finish softly
