Privacy policy

QuickCues: Interview Producer, previously named On Cue.
Effective November 20, 2026. Covers the current iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps, including TestFlight.

Your library and offline use

QuickCues stores your active interview details, questions, sound bites, templates and preferences on your device. Basic generation uses the included library on your device. Premium can also use that library when offline. QuickCues does not record, transcribe or listen to interviews, and it does not request microphone, contacts or device-location access.

The current set and its title can transfer automatically to your paired Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. The Watch can keep that set for offline use and use its own local library.

Optional usage analytics

The iPhone and iPad app uses Mixpanel to understand feature use and improve reliability. Starting with build 32, usage analytics is on when no preference has been saved. An existing saved off or on choice is preserved. Earlier builds used an off-by-default setting.

Analytics includes feature and category selections, question and sound-bite counts, generation attempts and outcomes, error categories, timing ranges, offline fallback, feedback counts, and save, template and interview-mode activity. Custom category names are sent only as “Custom.” Mixpanel also receives a random identifier for this installation, event timestamps, app and SDK versions, operating system, device model and manufacturer, screen dimensions, and available connection-type or carrier metadata.

We do not send interview details, names, company or event text, typed locations, custom category text, questions, sound bites, recordings, Apple account details or purchase account identifiers to Mixpanel. The analytics identifier groups activity from one installation; we do not use it to identify you across devices or connect it to your Apple or purchase account. We disable IP-based geolocation, advertising-identifier collection, automatic event tracking, feature flags and session replay.

You can turn off Share usage analytics in Settings → Privacy & analytics. This stops new app analytics collection and discards unsent app-side events. It does not recall data already transmitted, including a request already in transit. Analytics is optional and turning it off does not remove app features. Mixpanel's handling of received data is described in its privacy information.

Apple sign-in and purchases

Basic does not require an account. Connecting with Apple for Premium authenticates a QuickCues account through Supabase. Apple provides an app-specific identity and may provide the email address or private relay address you authorize. This flow requests email access, not your full name. We use the account identifier to authenticate requests and connect your Premium access across devices.

Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription records, product and entitlement status, transaction information and an app customer identifier so we can verify, restore and manage Premium access and understand subscription activity. After sign-in, that identifier is your QuickCues account ID. We disable RevenueCat's automatic device-identifier collection and optional diagnostics. We do not send interview content to RevenueCat or receive your payment-card or bank details. See RevenueCat's privacy information and Supabase's privacy policy.

Premium cloud generation

When you choose to generate tailored results with Premium while online, your current filming type, purpose, guest role, company, event or topic, applicable product and timing details, and optional context are sent to the QuickCues service hosted by Supabase and then to OpenAI. The optional Location field is included if you fill it in. It can describe a city or a more precise place you type; QuickCues does not obtain your device's location.

Generate More and replacement requests may also send up to 30 lines from the current set to help avoid repeats. Other saved library items and the separate interviewee Name field are not included in the cloud-generation request. Anything you put in the other shared fields or current lines can nevertheless contain names or other personal information. Only include information you want these services to process.

The QuickCues service verifies your account, checks your RevenueCat entitlement and enforces usage limits. It stores account and request identifiers, request times and completion outcomes for access control, rate limits and duplicate prevention. Its application diagnostics contain technical stages, error categories, counts, timing and token usage, not your brief or generated text. The generation handler does not save prompts or answers in the QuickCues usage database or its application diagnostic events. Hosting services also process network and security metadata, such as IP addresses, to operate the service.

OpenAI processes the submitted context and produces suggestions. We set the Responses API's store option to false. This does not mean zero retention: OpenAI's separate abuse-monitoring logs may contain prompts, responses and related metadata. Its documented default is retention for up to 30 days, with possible longer retention for legal or safety reasons. We do not claim a special zero-retention arrangement. See OpenAI's API data controls.

Optional private iCloud sync

If you enable sync, saved questions, sound bites, sets, templates and preferences are stored as encrypted content fields in the app's private CloudKit database under your iCloud account. These records can contain the details and names you saved with a set or template. Sync is separate from Premium generation and Mixpanel analytics. Turning sync off or signing out keeps your local library and stops subsequent syncing; it does not erase existing iCloud records.

Deleting an item removes it from the current library through a synced deletion record. Earlier versions can remain in the sync history. Apple device backups and iCloud may also retain app data according to your Apple settings. Manage those copies through Apple's storage and backup controls. See Apple's privacy information.

Retention and your choices

Local drafts and saved items remain until you edit or remove them or remove the app's stored data. Clearing the active Details does not delete saved questions or templates. Signing out does not delete a server account, subscription records or previously received analytics. The app does not set a fixed automatic deletion deadline for those service records. Provider retention and backup rules also apply; a rolling generation allowance is not a promise to delete older request records.

For questions, access or deletion requests, contact hello@cerious.com. We may need enough information to locate the relevant account or installation and verify the request. Some transaction, security or legal records may need to be retained. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups or data already received by a provider.

Content you choose to share

When you use the native share sheet, the text you select is passed to the app or destination you choose. That destination's privacy practices apply. Local diagnostic summaries are shared only when you choose to export them.

No advertising tracking

QuickCues does not display ads, use advertising identifiers, sell interview content, or combine app data with other companies' data for targeted advertising or advertising measurement. The services described above are used for app functionality, tailored generation, purchase management, analytics and reliability.

Support requests

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to and resolve your request. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, tracking scripts, cookies or forms to this website. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the app services described above.

Contact and updates

QuickCues is provided by Michael Cerdeiros. For privacy questions, contact hello@cerious.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date.
