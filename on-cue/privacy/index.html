Privacy policy

Applies to On Cue: Interview Producer version 1.0.
Effective November 14, 2026.

Information in the app

On Cue processes the interview brief and prepares questions on your device. The app stores the current brief, saved custom category names and saved question cards locally using Apple’s app storage. It does not send these contents to a server operated by the developer.

No account, tracking or recording

The current app does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, make network requests, or access your microphone. It does not record, transcribe or actively listen to interviews. Question preparation is an offline feature, not a cloud AI service.

Content you choose to share

When you use the native share sheet, the text you select is passed to the app or destination you choose. That destination’s privacy practices apply to its handling of your shared content.

Local storage and Apple services

You can remove saved question cards from Saved with swipe-to-delete. Editing a brief replaces the current brief. Custom category names remain saved in this version unless the app’s stored data is removed. Device backups and Apple services may retain app data according to your Apple settings. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups.

Support requests

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to and resolve your request. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, tracking scripts, cookies or forms. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its privacy statement. The website’s hosting is separate from the offline app.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact hello@cerious.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
