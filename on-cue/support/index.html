Support

QuickCues helps you prepare questions for video interviews. Choose a scenario, describe the interview, and work through a set of prompts. Version 1.0 works offline and does not record, listen to, or transcribe an interview.

Your questions and categories

Use the interview brief to change the context of your questions. You can add a custom category name and reuse it later. Saved question cards appear in Saved; swipe a card to delete it. Editing the brief replaces the current brief. Version 1.0 does not provide a separate delete control for custom category names.

Sharing

The share button opens Apple’s share sheet for the selected question and follow-up text. You choose the receiving app or person.

Get help

Email hello@cerious.com.

For a bug report, include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that caused the issue. Avoid sending confidential client details or interview content unless needed for your support request.
